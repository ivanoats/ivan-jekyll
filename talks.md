## 2026

**Why CharTiles?** CUGOS lightning talk, November 2026. ([slides](https://chartiles.com/cugos/))
Why adding nautical chart features to a web map takes more work than adding an OpenStreetMap basemap, and what a coastline that vanished during tiling taught me about checking a data pipeline at every stage.

**One Sea, 23 Ferry Operators, Zero Maps.** CUGOS lightning talk, October 2026. ([slides](https://salishseaferrymap.com/talks/cugos))
How the [Salish Sea Ferry Map](https://salishseaferrymap.com) put every ferry route in the region on one map.

Both are 20-slide Ignite talks at [CUGOS](https://cugos.org), the Cascadia Users of Geospatial Open Source.
