I build AI-assisted data pipelines for maps, charts, and media, with a check at every stage. I also help software teams adopt AI-assisted development without giving up review or ownership. Seattle, thinking from the Salish Sea.

## Currently

- Building [CharTiles](https://chartiles.com), an open-source pipeline that turns NOAA nautical charts into tiles you can click and inspect, served from static storage with no tile server.
- Maintaining [Salish Sea Ferry Map](https://salishseaferrymap.com): every ferry route in the region on one map, with more than 138,000 views since September.
- Building [wsg-check](https://wsg-check.com), a checker for the [W3C Web Sustainability Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/TR/web-sustainability-guidelines/) that also runs as an MCP server inside AI assistants.
- Designing [Verdant](https://verdant-design.org), a small design system with sustainable defaults. This site runs on it.
- Speaking at [CUGOS](https://cugos.org) about open-source maps and charts. The [CharTiles talk](https://chartiles.com/cugos/) is online.
- Taking a small number of consulting engagements: [how to work with me](/work-with-me/).

## Background

30 years of building on the web. Founded two web hosting companies, including Sustainable Websites in 2004, and two technical training companies. Taught at the University of Washington and [Code Fellows](https://www.codefellows.org/). FAA Part 107 drone pilot. Currently a Senior Solutions Architect at lululemon, working on content pipelines, digital asset management, and AI-assisted development.
