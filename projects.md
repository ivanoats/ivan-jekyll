Most of these started because I wanted the thing to exist and couldn't find it. They're also how I test ideas before I recommend them to anyone else. Source for nearly all of them is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ivanoats).

## Maps and charts

**[CharTiles](https://chartiles.com)**: NOAA nautical charts as tiles you can click and inspect. Chart imagery shows you the picture of a buoy or a wreck. CharTiles shows you what NOAA recorded about it, from PMTiles served by static storage with no tile server. ([source](https://github.com/ivanoats/chartiles), [talk](https://chartiles.com/cugos/))

**[Salish Sea Ferry Map](https://salishseaferrymap.com)**: every ferry route across the Salish Sea on one map: Washington State Ferries, BC Ferries, Black Ball, Kitsap Transit, the Victoria Clipper, and the county ferries. The routes are split across operators and two countries; this puts them in one place. More than 138,000 views. ([source](https://github.com/ivanoats/Salish-Sea-Ferry-Map), [talk](https://salishseaferrymap.com/talks/cugos))

**[Northwest Discovery Water Trail map](https://ndwt-ol-chakra.netlify.app/)**: an interactive map for paddlers, with topographic and nautical basemaps you can switch between. ([source](https://github.com/ivanoats/ndwt-ol-chakra))

## Sustainable web

**[wsg-check](https://wsg-check.com)**: checks a website against the [W3C Web Sustainability Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/TR/web-sustainability-guidelines/). It runs on the web, as a command-line tool that can fail a CI build, and as an MCP server so AI assistants can check a site, fix it, and check again. ([source](https://github.com/ivanoats/wsg-check))

**[Verdant](https://verdant-design.org)**: a small design system with sustainable defaults. Its demo site loads in 52.6 KiB on a first visit. This site, the ferry map, and Good Paddle all use it. ([source](https://github.com/ivanoats/verdant-wsg-demo))

**[Rendition](https://github.com/ivanoats/rendition)**: an open-source media CDN in Rust that resizes and converts images on request, as an alternative to commercial image services.

**[Sustainable Websites](https://sustainablewebsites.com)**: my web sustainability consulting site, a revival of the green web host I started in 2004.

## On the water

**[West Point Wind](https://westpointwind.com)**: wind and tide data for people in kayaks and on paddleboards.

**[How Fast Where](https://howfastwhere.com)**: shows your speed and position while you're moving.

**[Good Paddle](https://goodpaddle.com)**: stand-up paddleboard instruction and coastal exploration.

**[StorckPhotos](https://storckphotos.com)**: commercial architecture, drone, and outdoor photography.
